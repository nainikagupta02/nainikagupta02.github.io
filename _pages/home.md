I’m a healthcare data scientist, currently based in Chicago.

I earned my M.S. in Precision Health (Data Science & AI track) at the University of Chicago, and my B.Tech. in Biological Systems Engineering at Plaksha University. I’m curious about how biological systems work and how we can use data and mathematical models to understand them better.

My work spans cancer research, environmental epidemiology, and mechanistic modelling of biological systems. I’ve worked with MRI and histopathology images, RNA-seq, electronic health records (EHR), and Medicare claims to study disease biology, survival, and healthcare risk. I’ve also recently completed HIPAA Privacy and Security and CITI Human Subjects Research training.
