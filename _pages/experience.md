## Student Researcher

**[Center for Computational Medicine & Clinical Artificial Intelligence](https://biomedai.bsd.uchicago.edu/), The University of Chicago**  
PI: Dr. Madeleine Torcasso  
May–August 2026

- Built a containerized Nextflow/Python pipeline for prostate histopathology, combining color deconvolution, Cellpose nuclear segmentation, and quantitative nuclear shape features on an HPC cluster.

## Undergraduate Thesis Researcher

**[Cancer Systems Biology Lab](https://be.iisc.ac.in/~mkjolly/), IISc Bangalore**  
PI: Dr. Mohit Kumar Jolly  
January–May 2025

- Developed two 2D agent-based models in R to simulate epithelial–mesenchymal spatial dynamics, including population growth, neighborhood interactions, and emergent spatial patterns.
- Used Gillespie stochastic simulation with density-dependent growth and Gaussian-weighted interaction fields to study competition under crowding. A second model explored adhesion-mediated migration through contact formation, contact breaking, and neighbor-biased movement.
- Simulations showed epithelial cells overcoming crowding inhibition to outcompete mesenchymal cells, and epithelial adhesion suppressing mesenchymal migration.

## Summer Intern

**[Biomolecular Engineering Lab](https://sites.google.com/iitb.ac.in/ganesh/research?authuser=0), Department of Chemical Engineering, IIT Bombay**  
PI: Dr. Ganesh A. Viswanathan  
June–August 2023

- Developed a Boolean network model of TNF-induced cell death pathways spanning 32 species and 21 reactions, integrating NF-κB survival signaling with apoptosis, pyroptosis, and necroptosis.
- Computed logical steady states across TNF input conditions and simulated three targeted inhibitors (Takinib, Z-VAD, and Nec-1s) using CellNetAnalyzer in MATLAB. In the model, blocking one pathway redirected cells to another death pathway; all three inhibitors were needed to prevent cell death.
